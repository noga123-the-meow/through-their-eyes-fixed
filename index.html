Why It Matters

People with disabilities such as color blindness and dyslexia experience the world in unique ways that others may not always notice. Normal activities, such as matching a shirt with leggings, may seem easy to most people but can be difficult or even impossible for someone who is colorblind. While reading and processing words is something many people learn by third grade, people with dyslexia often struggle just to keep up. These challenges can affect everyday activities, learning, and even using websites or apps. By understanding these experiences, we can create more accessible designs and build a world that is more inclusive, supportive, and fair for everyone.
